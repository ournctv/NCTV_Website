Regional community media organization recognizes Morris for building an award-winning sports broadcasting program that has transformed local sports coverage and inspired a new generation of broadcasters.

NANTUCKET, MA – Chris Morris, the driving force behind Nantucket Community Television’s Nantucket Sports Connection, has been selected to receive the 2026 Rika Welsh Community Impact Award from the Alliance for Community Media - Northeast Region (ACM-NE).

The award recognizes an individual or group that has generated a project using Public, Educational, and Government (PEG) media resources to advance awareness and understanding of an important cause. Morris was selected in recognition of his extraordinary contributions to the Nantucket community through the creation and development of the Nantucket Sports Connection, NCTV’s live sports broadcasting initiative.

The award will be formally presented at the ACM-NE Special Award Luncheon on Tuesday, November 17, 2026, during the organization’s annual Fall Conference in Nashua, New Hampshire.

What began approximately five years ago as a modest independent production effort has grown into one of Nantucket Community Television’s most successful and recognizable programming initiatives. Working initially with little more than a camera, a microphone, and a borrowed laptop running a shareware program, Morris and NSC co-founder Sean Davis established a live sports coverage operation that has fundamentally changed how Nantucket’s student-athletes, families, and community experience local sports.

In the fall of 2023, NCTV brought the Nantucket Sports Connection in-house as a dedicated department, providing an institutional foundation for its continued growth. Since its inception, the program has accumulated more than 300,000 views on YouTube and over 60,000 hours of watch time – remarkable numbers for a community serving a high school with an annual graduating class of approximately 150 students.

The impact extends well beyond viewership statistics. Student-athletes have used NSC footage to create highlight reels that have helped them pursue opportunities at NCAA Division I programs, as well as competitive broadcasting and journalism programs. Students have also gained hands-on production experience as members of the NSC broadcast crews, with some inspired to pursue broadcasting as a career.

The program’s popularity has translated into extraordinary community support. Surveys of Nantucket residents and students have consistently identified the Nantucket Sports Connection as NCTV’s most important programming initiative and among the services they most want to see expanded. Originally covering eight sports teams, the program has nearly doubled its team coverage over the past two years.

The Commonwealth of Massachusetts has also recognized the program’s significance, allocating $30,000 in its most recent state budget specifically to support the work of the Nantucket Sports Connection – an exceptional achievement for a community media initiative.

“Chris took an idea and built something that has become enormously important to this community,” said NCTV station manager Charles Douglas, who nominated Morris for the award. “What started with borrowed equipment and a tremendous amount of personal initiative has grown into a program that connects our community, gives student-athletes greater visibility, provides students with real-world broadcasting experience, and has become a model of what community television can accomplish. This recognition is richly deserved.”

Morris’s influence has also extended beyond Nantucket. NCTV shares NSC broadcasts with other community media stations, and livestream links are shared with visiting teams traveling to Nantucket, allowing their home communities to follow their athletes’ performances even when their own local media outlets cannot provide coverage. NCTV has also shared the story of the Nantucket Sports Connection with other community media organizations at the national level, encouraging stations to develop similar locally focused sports broadcasting initiatives.

As Morris relocates to Maine with his family to conclude his five years of service, NCTV has taken steps to ensure that the program he helped build will continue to thrive. The organization recently hired sports broadcaster Patrick Scanlon, a graduate of Syracuse University’s prestigious Newhouse School of Public Communications, to carry the program forward. Morris has embraced the transition, demonstrating the same commitment to mentorship and collaboration that has characterized his work throughout his tenure.

“Chris is irreplaceable, but his legacy is something we shall carry forward,” Douglas added. “He has helped establish something that belongs to this community, and we are committed to making sure the Nantucket Sports Connection continues to grow and serve future generations of Whalers athletes and Nantucket families.”

In anticipation of Morris’s departure, NCTV also produced a special five-minute thank-you video featuring testimonials from colleagues, school administrators, and students whose lives and experiences have been touched by his work. The 2026 Rika Welsh Community Impact Award recognizes not simply a successful television program, but the lasting community impact that can result when local people are given the tools and opportunity to tell their own stories. For Nantucket, the Nantucket Sports Connection has become precisely that: a community institution built through initiative, collaboration, and a shared commitment to celebrating local student-athletes.
