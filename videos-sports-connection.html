The Island Cup - Nantucket Whalers Football vs. Martha’s Vineyard - October 31, 2026

The Nantucket Sports Connection proudly presents the spookiest showdown yet between these cross-island foes, as the Whalers square off against the Vineyarders on a Halloween Day Island Cup!

This is one of the most storied rivalries in all of Massachusetts sports, and the 77th meeting overall between these two programs, with Nantucket owning a 41-32-3 advantage coming in.

Last year’s Island Cup was supposed to be played on Martha’s Vineyard, but a last-minute decision by Coach Tony Mottola forfeited the game and the rest of the Vineyarders’ season, and was recorded in the MIAA Power Rankings as a 14-0 default victory by Nantucket.

Mottola since decided to depart the island to be head coach at Bonita Springs High School in Southwest Florida, handing the reigns to Zeke Vought, who’s served as an assistant coach since 2018. Nantucket likewise has a new coach at the helm, with former CPS coach Fernando Jones leading the Whalers this season.

The prior Island Cup in 2024 was one for the ages as the teams travelled to Fenway Park and slugged their way to a 14-14 tie at the end of regulation, with the Vineyarders missing a 26-yard field goal with two seconds left in the fourth quarter; the sudden death overtime that followed saw Arann Hanlon run in both the 8-yard touchdown and the two point conversion to give the Whalers a 22-14 victory. Naturally this game was the most viewed in the history of the NSC: https://youtube.com/live/1P3DZywTnR4

These two teams last met at Vito Capizzo in 2023, another contest that came down to the final seven seconds of the contest, with Martha’s Vineyard kicking an incredible 38-yard field goal to cap a comeback victory after a two-touchdown deficit late in the third quarter in front of a big Nantucket crowd: https://youtube.com/live/jthWbKobYVI

The last time the Whalers travelled with the cup was in 2022, when they similarly lost a heartbreaker 14-13, breaking a 5-game Nantucket winning streak. Overall the The Island Cup has been won 22 times by the Vineyarders and 23 times by the Whalers since 1978. This game always represents more than a football game, so much so that NCTV led by Frank Schadt made an award-winning documentary about it: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lftcwt6wXJk

MIAA Division 4 Round of 32: Nantucket Whalers Boys Lacrosse vs. Monomoy Sharks - June 1, 2026

To begin our Monday MIAA Tournament doubleheader, the Nantucket Sports Connection proudly presents the Whalers Varsity Boys Lacrosse squad beginning the march to their third consecutive Final Four appearance with a rematch against their league opponent Monomoy Sharks.

Nantucket (15-3) is ranked #2 in Division 4 and enters their seventh consecutive postseason tournament with a squad featuring 13 seniors, most of whom were on the 2024 state championship team. Their only losses on the season were against tough higher division teams from Nauset, Falmouth & Shrewsbury.

Monomoy (6-13) squeaked in at #31 and had to face a preliminary round competitor from #34 Monson, who came out to the Cape from Western Mass on a stormy Sunday afternoon looking for an upset, forcing Monomoy into overtime before falling 8-7.

The Sharks have yet to score a single goal on the Whalers this season, falling by a combined score of 33-0 in their pair of regular season faceoffs, the latest of their seven consecutive losses in head-to-head play. While managing a single road victory in 2018, the Sharks have yet to win after crossing the water.

The Whalers graduated six seniors at the end of a dramatic tournament run last year. After a 12-5 regular season record, Nantucket entered the Division 4 playoffs as the #4 seed, sweeping aside Lenox, Sandwich and Ipswich in succession before once again facing their longtime nemesis, the Skippers, who bested them 11-2 before falling to Norwell in the state title game. This was the third time Cohasset has knocked Nantucket out of postseason play, having done so before in 2015 & 2019 — but due to the bracket structure this year, the Whalers will not face the Skippers all the way through the tournament until the state title game.

Live coverage begins at 1:45 with the first draw scheduled for 2pm from Vito Capizzo Stadium, coming to you live both on the NSC YouTube channel as well as on NCTV HD Channel 1072.
