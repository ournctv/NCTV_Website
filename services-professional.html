Professional Services

NCTV specializes in filling the audio/visual needs of Nantucket organizations in a cost-efficient and detail-oriented way.

Our Services

To learn how we can make your next event a success and grow its impact through video & social media, please email our Senior Producer Zac Visco at zac@ournctv.org.

Our services include:

  • Filming, editing, and broadcasting videos of your presentation, concert, symposium, ceremony, etc.
  • Live streaming your event to social media (including YouTube) as well as TV
  • Creating promotional videos
  • Podcast and voice-over recordings (including recordings in which guests are remote)
  • Video podcast recordings
  • Facilitating “hybrid” meetings in which some participants are physically present and others attend virtually.
  • Studio recordings with green screen capability.
  • Producing, filming, and editing social media-optimized content like Instagram Stories & Reels
  • Drone videography
  • Providing audio/visual services for events. These services include providing microphones, electronic speakers, screens, &/or the staff to operate them.
